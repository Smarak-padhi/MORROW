# MORROW Story Bible

## Premise
Morrow is a young Stitchling with a magical needle that can join impossible things. A clockwork inspector, Professor Primrose Perfect, uses the Great Correction Clock to erase irregularities in shadows, weather and time. Morrow and the visitor repair three places, then discover the shadowy helper is Morrow's rejected first creation, the Unfinished One.

## Cast
- Morrow: clumsy, curious, gentle apprentice repairer; learns mistakes are not necessarily wrong.
- Sir Clack: sarcastic talking toolbox, dad-joke enthusiast; secretly keeps a scrapbook of Morrow's achievements.
- Pipkin: dramatic courier bird; writes The Daily Panic and fears becoming unnecessary.
- Professor Primrose Perfect: theatrical, rule-obsessed antagonist; was rejected as imperfect and fears future mistakes.
- Unfinished One: abandoned patchwork shadow, secretly rescues discarded things and helps Morrow.

## Chapters
Prologue: wake Morrow, fix workshop sign. Chapter 1: recover shadows, experiment with duck/tree combinations. Chapter 2: restore rain and weather, accidentally inconvenience a frog. Chapter 3: restart clock using earlier skills. Finale: Unfinished One reveals itself; Perfect's past is explained; player creates an unexpected combination, and Morrow independently makes final stitch. Epilogue: three inventions on portfolio shelf, scrapbook reveal, honorary repairer certificate.

## Voice
Humor is affectionate, not mean-spirited. Sir Clack's puns and Pipkin's newspaper punctuate story scenes. Emotional beats should not be drowned out by jokes. Morrow's final lesson: 'We make wonderful mistakes.'
