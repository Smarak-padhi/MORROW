## 1. Visual Register & Creative Direction

### The "Living Miniature" Philosophy
MORROW must feel like a tangible physical diorama captured with high-end macro stop-motion cinematography (in the style of Laika Studios and Wes Anderson's *Fantastic Mr. Fox*). Every surface must exhibit visible handcrafted texture: needle-punched wool felt, frayed burlap, carved weathered timber, aged tarnished brass, hand-stitched embroidery seams, and fine porcelain kintsugi.

### Core Visual Principles
1. **Material Truth**: No generic digital CGI or flat vectors. If an asset is fabric, we see the weave and loose fibers. If it is metal, we see machining grooves and patina.
2. **Atmospheric Lighting**: Warm, directional tungsten studio lighting with shallow depth of field, soft dust motes, and dramatic cast shadows.
3. **Character Silhouette & Scale**: Morrow is small (~12–15 cm tall in real-world scale), Sir Clack is dense and sturdy, Duck is plump and stubborn, Frog Mayor is dignified yet damp, Professor Perfect is austere and brittle, and The Unfinished One is rough-hewn but lovable.
