## 1. Core Cast Specifications

### 1. Morrow (The Apprentice Stitchling)
- **Role:** Protagonist, apprentice repairer of impossible things.
- **Physical Identity:** Pear-shaped fantasy creature crafted from peach wool felt (`#E8B28C` / `#BA7D69`).
- **Head & Face:**
  - Asymmetric folded petal ears (left ear perked upright with gold lining `#E8C878`, right ear flopped forward).
  - Stitched gold diamond marking centered on forehead (`#E8C878`).
  - Black antique button eyes with white four-hole cross-thread twinkle (`#FFF8F1`).
  - Tiny hand-stitched mouth; curious, earnest, and slightly crooked smile.
- **Body & Limbs:**
  - Round pear-shaped torso without neck.
  - Peach felt mitten paws (NO claws, NO five-fingered human hands, NO opposable thumbs).
  - Short rounded felt feet anchored by contact shadow.
- **Outfit & Accessories:**
  - Oversized chunky plum patchwork knitted scarf (`#8E79A8` & `#A34063`) wrapped twice around neck with trailing fringed end.
  - Spool tail: Flexible woven cord tail ending in a polished wooden sewing spool/bobbin (`#E8C878`) trailing glowing gold thread.
  - Carrying tool: Giant polished brass tapestry needle with glowing golden eye.
- **Acting & Motion:**
  - Gentle, impulsive, clumsy, bobbing walk cycle; ears react dynamically to sound; scarf flutters with motion.

### 2. Sir Clack (The Sentient Toolbox)

- **Physical Identity:** Weathered antique mahogany wooden toolbox (`#8F5D48` / `#63392A`) with brass hinges, latches, and handle.
- **Facial Features:**
  - Heavy carved wooden brow permanently furrowed into a deadpan, unimpressed stare.
  - Dark round brass-plate eyes with tiny white highlights.
  - Brass keyhole mouth slot resembling a stoic mustache.
- **Details:**
  - Polished brass corner brackets (`#E8C878`) with antique rivets.
  - Single horizontal drawer divider seam across lower third.
  - Overhead arched brass and rosewood carrying handle (`#C98F70`).
  - Inside: Sir Clack's "Disaster Log" — which is secretly an embossed leather scrapbook of Morrow's proudest mistakes.
- **Acting & Motion:**
  - Snaps twin brass latches like jaws when emphasizing points; pivots slowly on base; shakes subtly with exasperation.

## 2. Scale, Layering & Scene Continuity

```
Height Scale Hierarchy (Relative to Scene Floor):
[Duck: 100px]  <  [Morrow: 120px]  <  [Sir Clack: 130px]  <  [Frog Mayor: 135px]  <  [Professor Perfect: 210px]
Pipkin (Flying/Perched): 45px body width.
```

- **Persistence Rule:** Characters NEVER vanish or change proportions between video playback, dialogue turns, and interactive puzzle phases.
- **Shadow Rule:** All characters share the same light source (warm amber twilight lanterns in Village; filtered top green mist in Forest; overhead gaslamp in Town).
- **Z-Index Layering in Scene Canvas:**
  1. `z-index: 1`: Sky, distant roofline silhouette, stars/clouds.
  2. `z-index: 2`: Midground architecture, cobblestone ground plane.
  3. `z-index: 3`: Cast shadows (Duck's tree shadow, character contact shadows).
  4. `z-index: 4`: Characters (Sir Clack left, Pipkin perched, Duck center-right, Morrow right).
  5. `z-index: 5`: Interactive seams / golden repair thread canvas.
  6. `z-index: 6`: Speech balloons and dialog reactions.

## 3. Strict Rejection Criteria (For Video Prompts & Code)

1. **NO Disney Fox / Anthropomorphic Animals:** Morrow is an original fantasy Stitchling creature, NEVER a fox, cat, bear, or dog.
2. **NO Human Fingers:** Morrow's hands are round peach felt mitten paws. Never generate hands with 5 individual fingers or human skin texture.
3. **NO Duplicate Duck Shadows:** Duck must NEVER have both a duck shadow and a tree shadow. The tree shadow IS Duck's shadow.
4. **NO Photorealistic CGI / Plastic 3D:** Everything must appear handcrafted: wool felt, hand-knitted yarn, paper-cut cardstock, carved rosewood, and polished brass.
5. **NO Unanchored Dialogue:** Speech balloons must visibly point toward the active speaking character with an anchor tail.
6. **NO Full-Screen Lockouts:** Cinematic clips must fit within the storybook layout, allowing immediate pause, mute, or skip.
